# Navbar

Fixed top `.holo-glass` + `.holo-nav` bar: frosted ice glass with a drifting iridescent sheen and frost grain. Chromatic wordmark. Tabs use a hover halo (no bottom-border underlines). Links use Lenis `scrollTo` to anchors: Story, Abilities, Skins, End. Mobile collapses to a holo sheet menu. Persistent across the whole scroll.
