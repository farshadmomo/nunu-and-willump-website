# PRODUCT

register: brand

## Product Purpose

A single-page cinematic tribute site for the League of Legends champion **Nunu & Willump**, a boy and his yeti from the Freljord. The site is not a utility. It is an experience: the visitor scrolls through a scripted, scroll-driven story that mirrors the champion fantasy (a snowball that grows as it rolls, abilities revealed inside the snowball, a gallery of skins, a playful willump bite that ends the ride). Design IS the product here. Every scroll beat should feel authored, theatrical, and frost-bitten.

## Users

- League of Legends players and fans who already love this champion and want fan service done with craft.
- Champion-spotlight / portfolio viewers judging the build on polish and motion quality.
- People arriving from a link with no context, who should be pulled in within the first scroll.

Usage context: desktop-first, leaned-back, "show me something" mood. Mobile must still tell the full story, gracefully degraded. Sound is optional / muted by default.

## Tone & Voice

Wonder over hype. Cold, crystalline, and a little magical, like standing inside a snow globe at dusk. Copy is sparse and storybook-tinged ("a boy and his yeti", "maybe they will be heroes after all"). Never gamer-bro, never marketing-shouty, never SaaS-cheerful.

## Strategic Principles

1. **No lag, ever.** Everything heavy (hero frames, skins, ability loops) preloads behind the loading snowball. The reveal only happens at 100%. Jank breaks the spell.
2. **The scroll is the narrative.** Forward motion always advances the story; nothing is a static dump. One clear beat per section.
3. **Frost as a material, not a filter.** Glass, iridescence, and prism light are the physical world of the Freljord, used with intent, not sprinkled as decoration.
4. **Earn the wow, then get out of the way.** Reserve the loudest effects (chromatic title, prism sweep, snowball fill) for hero moments. Lore and ability text stay legible and calm.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)

- Generic dark-blue-neon "gaming" landing pages. The first-reflex Freljord look (navy + glowing cyan on black) is the trap. Go iridescent oil-slick on cracked ice instead.
- SaaS template energy: hero-metric blocks, identical icon+heading+text card grids, purple-gradient-on-white.
- Esports-org corporate sites with stock angular shapes and aggressive italic everything.
- Riot's own official pages copied beat for beat. Take inspiration, not layout.

## Reflex-reject check

If someone could guess the palette from "ice champion" alone (navy + neon cyan), it failed first-order. If they could guess it from "ice champion but not navy-neon" (so, frosted white minimalism), that is the second-order trap. The answer is holo-iridescent oil-slick on tinted near-black ice, which neither reflex predicts.
